## 4. Method caveats, stated plainly

- **Bundle reduction.** OpenRouter imposed a prompt-cost ceiling. The final bundle carried the
  complete code and configuration diff plus the current policy and evidence documents, but excluded
  the remainder of `docs/` from the monolithic diff. No application code, configuration or test file
  was withheld from review.
- **Provider failures do not count.** OpenRouter returned HTTP 402 on the full bundle and at a
  22 000-token ceiling; Moonshot returned HTTP 429 (account suspended). Neither is treated as
  evidence in either direction.
- **Static review.** Neither model executed code, inspected the installed dependency tree, or probed
  a deployment. Their findings are leads that were reproduced or falsified here by direct execution
  and inspection; both models' `unable_to_verify` lists are preserved in the raw outputs.
- **Raw outputs retained** under `~/.hermes/cache/scratch/ai-review-299b995/` and
  `ai-review-299b995-mini/` (parsed copies alongside).
