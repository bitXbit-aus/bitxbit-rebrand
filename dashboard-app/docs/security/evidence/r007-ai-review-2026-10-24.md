## 7. Residual risks for owner decision

1. **R-002's native-addon exclusion is unverified as an effective control** (non-discriminating check;
   no positive control against the Linux artifact). Flagged by both models as the top structural gap.
   The measured live protection remains the `Buffer.from` argument guard at the reachable caller.
2. **Browser paths are unexercised:** PKCE sign-in/verifier/callback exchange, browser cookie writing,
   and wallet connect/disconnect/verification under React 19 with a nested React 18 copy present.
3. **Refreshed-request-cookie forwarding has no adapter-level regression test**, and repeated-`setAll`
   preservation has no direct executable coverage.
4. **Redirect cookie verification is not fully browser-equivalent** — it decodes and matches the user,
   but does not merge deletion/expiry/domain/path/chunk-name semantics into the original jar.
5. **The page-refresh check proves an authenticated page rendered**, not uniquely which layer refreshed.
6. **IPv6 privacy-address rotation** can grant one physical client multiple rate-limit buckets;
   unresolved clients share the fail-closed `anonymous` bucket.
7. **Cookie writes outside middleware and the PKCE callback** cannot apply Supabase's cache headers via
   the `cookies()` API.
8. **GitHub does not enforce this review process** — `main` requires no approving review and
   auto-deploys, so process discipline is the control.
